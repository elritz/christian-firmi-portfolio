## The Hydrocut

<Lede>Singletrack at home in Kitchener-Waterloo.</Lede>

[The Hydrocut](https://www.thehydrocut.ca/) is a volunteer-built trail network just outside Kitchener, with about 35 km of continuous singletrack.

<Lede>Taking the bike abroad.</Lede>

## Vancouver

<Lede>Riding and hiking the hills around Vancouver.</Lede>
