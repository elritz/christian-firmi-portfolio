## Bringing padel to KWRC

<Lede>Padel is the first new sport at the club in twenty years, and I helped bring it in.</Lede>

I sat on the padel committee, which worked out what adding padel to KWRC would take. On the board, I worked to keep the project aligned with the rest of the club, so a new sport could grow alongside squash instead of competing with it.

## Court repairs

<Lede>Volunteered a lot of hours in 2025 repairing the doubles and singles courts. The singles lines went green, our club and city colour, which was my idea.</Lede>
