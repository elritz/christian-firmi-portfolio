## What Ivy is for

<Lede>A home for research communities, built around the questions people work on.</Lede>

Ivy is a network for researchers, organized by research topic. You start from the questions you work on, not a title: claim the topics, see who else is working on the same ones, and find the conferences where that work meets in person.

Behind the network, Ivy is a technology platform to manage both conferences and scientific societies, designed to facilitate community engagement, event discovery, and networking.

## Design discovery: the mark

<Lede>The name is settled. The mark is still in discovery.</Lede>

Before committing to a logo, I'm exploring directions against the places it has to live: app icons, favicons, the wordmark, and the product itself. Two directions have held up so far: a serif V with an inline stroke, and a single-stroke script V.

The sheet below is the working file, not a final. It tests each direction at icon and favicon sizes across iOS, Android, and macOS, pairs it with the wordmark, and drops it into phone previews to see how it reads in context.
