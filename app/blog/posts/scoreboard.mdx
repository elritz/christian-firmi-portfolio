Scoreboard is a live scoring app for racquet sports: badminton, padel, pickleball, squash, and tennis. Referee a match from your phone, go live on YouTube with the score overlaid on the broadcast, and let anyone watch with a six-character code or a QR scan. No account, no download.

##### Product Highlights

1. A referee screen built for the court: serve side, lets, strokes, appeals, faults, and undo/redo, in portrait or landscape
2. Go live on YouTube from the app with a real-time scoreboard overlaid on the stream; live scores start the moment you do
3. Spectators join in seconds with a 6-character event code or a QR scan, with nothing to install
4. Clubs get teams, events, members with roles, and a push notification the moment a match goes live
5. Basic, Pro, and Club memberships with in-app purchases for unlimited live events, streaming, and match history

## The App

### Referee

The referee view is the heart of the app. Big tappable scores, the court diagram with who is serving from which box, and one-tap calls for the decisions a referee actually makes: let, stroke, no let, fault, appeal, out. Undo and redo are always a thumb away, and the same screen works in landscape when the phone is on a tripod.

### Live for everyone

Going live is a single switch. Scoreboard streams to YouTube Live and paints the score on top of the broadcast, so parents, scouts, and fans watch from a link with no extra apps. Anyone at the club can also follow along in the app by entering the event code or scanning the QR on the referee's screen.

### Events and clubs

Matches group into events so results and stats stay together. A club is one home for its teams, events, and members, with roles for who can score, who can stream, and who just watches. Members get a notification the moment their club goes live, with who is playing and the sport.

### Membership

The app starts free and grows with the club. Pro unlocks unlimited live events, YouTube streaming, and unlimited history. Club adds push notifications to every subscriber the moment you go live.

## Design

### Order in the court

The mark is three slanted panels in teal, the kind you would flip on a court-side scoreboard. For the campaign I paired it with hand-painted squash players, mid-rally and mid-panic, because the app is serious about the score and the sport is still supposed to be fun.
