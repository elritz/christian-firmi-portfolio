### Summary

- Member of the Data Acquisition (DA) team focused on maintaining data collection during surveying.
- Built native iOS ARKit modules and integrated a proprietary spatial-measurement library to enable accurate interior measurements.
- Created templating scripts for navigation, components, and icons to accelerate development and enforce consistency.
- Led company-wide demos and engineering presentations for releases, features, and optimizations; wrote app and brand onboarding documentation.

### Tech Stack (selected)

- React Native, Swift, Kotlin, GO, c++

### Key Contributions

- Feature development
  - Authentication
  - iOS Mobile Capture
  - Cone of perception on the 2D map, showing where the active scan is looking
  - Scan number markers on the map and the interaction to jump between scans
  - Tags during capture, including tag creation
  - Settings, including the capture guidance controls
  - Firebase remote config
  - Sentry and Heap analytics
  - templating
- Modernization
  - Android edge to edge
  - Migrated to Redux for state management
  - Brought React Navigation standards to navigation heiarchy

#### Mobile Design

**A note on what you're looking at.** The screens below are a first draft I started on my own initiative, not the app as it ships today. When I join a company I learn the product, then redraw it as a way to collect my ideas, simplify what I see, and pin down what I think the app should focus on. Nobody asked for it; it is how I get my thinking out early, before I know the codebase well enough to be told what is hard. The current iGUIDE PLANIX app is on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/iguide-planix/id6450938233) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.planitar.iguide&hl=en_CA); screenshots of it are below so the two can be compared.
