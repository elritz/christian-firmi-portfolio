iGUIDE is a property visualization and measurement platform: 3D tours, accurate floor plans, and spatial data for real estate and property management. I'm on the Data Acquisition team, which owns the PLANIX app that operators use to survey a building.

## Role

<Lede>Data Acquisition team. Keep capture working in the field, and make it better.</Lede>

- Built native iOS ARKit modules and integrated a proprietary spatial-measurement library for accurate interior measurements
- Created templating scripts for navigation, components, and icons to speed development and keep it consistent
- Led company-wide demos and engineering presentations for releases, features, and optimizations
- Wrote the app and brand onboarding documentation

## What I built

<Lede>Features an operator touches on every survey, plus the modernization underneath them.</Lede>

### Features

- Cone of perception on the 2D map, showing where the active scan is looking
- Scan number markers on the map and the interaction to jump between scans
- Tags during capture, including tag creation
- Settings, including the capture guidance controls
- Authentication, iOS mobile capture, Firebase remote config, Sentry and Heap analytics

### Modernization

- Android edge-to-edge
- Migrated state management to Redux
- Brought the navigation hierarchy onto React Navigation standards
